# Hostel

The college provides separate hostel accommodation for eligible male and female students.

Hostel rooms are furnished with beds, study tables, chairs, wardrobes, and basic lighting.

The hostel provides 24-hour electricity and drinking water facilities.

Students have access to common rooms, Wi-Fi connectivity, and recreational areas.

The hostel also provides mess facilities serving breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and dinner.

Basic laundry facilities are available for hostel residents.

The campus hostel has security arrangements and restricted entry for visitors.

Hostel rooms are allocated based on availability and the college hostel admission process.

Students staying in the hostel must follow hostel rules and regulations.

Hostel fees are separate from academic tuition fees.

Hostel fees may vary depending on the type of accommodation and academic year.

Students should check the latest hostel notice for current fees, availability, and admission procedures.
